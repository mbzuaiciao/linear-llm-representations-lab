# Glossary

- **Activation / hidden state:** a model's vector at a token and layer.
- **Affine map:** `x↦Ax+b`; linear only when `b=0`.
- **Causal use:** changing a representation changes behavior through the proposed mechanism.
- **Concept direction:** a vector associated with a controlled semantic contrast.
- **Explained variance (`R²`):** improvement over predicting the target mean.
- **Jacobian:** the matrix of local first derivatives.
- **JVP:** Jacobian-vector product, the directional first-order change.
- **Linear probe:** a fitted linear/affine readout from activations to labels.
- **Low rank:** a matrix factored through fewer latent dimensions.
- **Participation ratio:** `(Σλ)²/Σλ²`, a soft spectral dimension summary.
- **Residual stream:** the vector channel updated additively across transformer blocks.
- **Regime:** a subset assigned its own local model.
- **Structural generalization:** success beyond interpolation near the fitting distribution.
