# Linear probes versus computation

A probe asks whether a researcher can recover `y` from `h` with `Wh+b`. It does not ask whether the model uses that subspace. Confounds include lexical shortcuts, correlated prompt templates, probe capacity, and nearly duplicated train/test sentences.

Stronger evidence combines template-held-out evaluation, selectivity controls, interventions that alter only the candidate feature, and downstream behavioral measurements. Even then, steering demonstrates sensitivity to an intervention, not necessarily the natural computation used by the unmodified model.
