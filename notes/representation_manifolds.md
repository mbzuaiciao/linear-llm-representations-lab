# Representation manifolds

The ambient residual stream is `R^d`, but natural prompts may visit a restricted set `M`. A nonlinear function can be simple on `M` and complicated away from it. PCA cumulative variance and participation ratio are lightweight summaries, not proof that activations form a smooth manifold or a definitive intrinsic-dimension estimate.

Fit on natural activations, then test on held-out natural data and calibrated off-distribution perturbations. A sharp off-manifold failure supports distribution-dependent simplicity, though poorly matched perturbations can exaggerate the effect.
